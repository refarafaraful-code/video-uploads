# Why our MW4 campaign clips stall at ~100-300 views (2026-11-04)

Owner report: the native batch (posted at 23:00 Israel and mornings, 3+ a day, correct hashtags) gets 100-300 views
per clip; the best one got ~300.

## Evidence
- Scraped 6 MW4 TikTok discover pages again (214 videos, `tiktok-scrape-2026-11-04-videos.json`). 14 of them are
  campaign posts like ours (disclosure tag + @Call of Duty + the official footage).
  - **Campaign posts: median 411 views.** Best: 4,728 views, from an account with 151K followers. The rest got 0-3,375.
  - **Every other MW4 post on the same pages: median 161,300 views.**
  - Campaign clippers copy the brief's example lines: "MW4 Multiplayer is looking unreal @Call of Duty #ad" appears on
    at least 3 accounts, and we used the same line.
- TikTok's guidelines (as quoted by secondary sources) make "reused or unoriginal content posted without creative edits"
  ineligible for the For You feed. Duplicate and template-matching detection is reported to flag near-identical
  videos even when they come from different accounts. 205 clippers post the same official footage.
  https://www.creativeainews.com/articles/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-rule-2026/
  https://snaptik-1ia.pages.dev/blog/why-tiktok-misflags-videos-how-to-appeal/
- Disclosure: one blog claims the paid-partnership toggle cuts reach 30-60%, but that is unverified; other sources
  say there is no difference. Posts that carry only hashtags and no toggle risk removal, so use the toggle.
  https://www.influencers-time.com/tiktok-paid-partnership-labeling-rules-now-throttle-reach/
- Platform-wide: TikTok content volume rose ~80% in early 2026 while views fell 31% (Metricool). Competition is
  heavier. https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-content-volume-up-nearly-80-percent-in-early-2026-as-per-post-performance-declined-report-finds/

## Diagnosis
1. The main cause is that the category itself underperforms, not our posting time or hashtags. Raw official footage
   that hundreds of accounts post at once looks duplicate / unoriginal to TikTok. The "native" winners (19.6M etc.)
   work because the footage is their own, often with a facecam or voice.
2. Our native batch was the least transformed thing we made: raw footage plus one line, and a caption copied from the
   brief. That is exactly the duplicate pattern.
3. New account plus Israel location plus 3+ similar posts a day: TikTok shows the first test batch to a small, mostly
   local audience.

## Changes
1. Transform every clip so it is clearly our own edit: a story or joke told through text across the whole clip,
   rankings and comparisons, meme framing, punch-ins on details, re-sequenced moments. Never post the footage nearly
   raw.
2. Add a human element. A real voice or facecam reaction is allowed (only AI voices are banned) and is the strongest
   differentiator. The owner can record a short English reaction or commentary.
3. Write unique captions; never reuse the brief's example lines.
4. Post 1-2 a day, the strongest and most different clips; do not post near-identical clips back to back.
5. Turn on TikTok's content disclosure toggle in addition to #Sponsored.
6. Check Analytics > Viewers > Locations. If it is mostly Israel, the account has an audience problem that editing
   cannot fix; keep posting consistently in English to build a US/EU audience (no VPN, per our policy).
