# Why clips stop at ~500 views (2026-11-07)

Owner report: across all formats, clips get 12-500 views and 1-9 likes; the best is ~470-500.

Evidence:
- Live campaign record (contentrewards API, 2026-11-06): MW4 S01 Trailer has 1,002 approved submissions and 12.17M total
  views from 218 creators, so the mean is ~12K, driven by a few outliers. Our scrapes of the campaign posts on
  TikTok discover pages (11-04 and 11-06) found medians of 179-411 views, with 1 post of 8 over 1K. **~450-500 views
  is normal to slightly above median for this campaign.**
- TikTok profile pages sit behind a login wall from this session, so other clippers' full post histories can't be read.
- Clipper guides (vendor sources): new accounts need a 10-20 post ramp; heavy campaign posting straight after
  creation is a spam signal; 1-3 clips a day per account; single niche. Clipping Culture bans posting the same video
  on multiple accounts. https://openclip.app/guides/clipping-on-tiktok https://www.ssemble.com/blog/account-warmup-guide-2026
- What does break out in this niche: creator voice (Season 1 explainers by tridzoid / tastyhusband: 120-350K) or a
  creator's own gameplay. Neither is possible with silent trailer re-edits alone, but a **real human voice is allowed**
  by the brief (only AI voice is banned).

Conclusion: more effects or other formats will not move this much. The levers left are (1) the owner's own English
voice-over with captions, (2) a steady cadence of 1-2 a day to finish the account ramp, (3) campaigns whose source is
less saturated (speech content where our captioning is an edge), and (4) the owner's own analytics (retention graph,
traffic source, viewer locations), which only the owner can see.
