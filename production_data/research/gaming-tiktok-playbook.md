# Gaming (CoD) TikTok playbook - research 2026-11-02

Method: web search only. tiktok.com, contentrewards.com and most blogs (opus.pro, eklipse, clipcreator) cannot be
opened from this cloud session, so this is built from search-result snippets. Like and view counts are page-level
aggregates, not per video. Most sources are tool-vendor blogs, so numbers are directional.

## What performs in the niche
- Music-synced edits: the most visible COD format. A 120fps Warzone edit (song + SFX) showed ~358K likes, and MW4
  edits built around one dialogue line showed ~510K likes on their discover page.
- Short, self-contained moments: a clutch, a standout sniper shot, a funny moment, a "watch till the end" payoff.
- Comment-prompt / POV formats: "POV:" overlays are common, and "rate it" / "which one" prompts drive comments.
- Clipping Culture's own MW4 campaigns reached 39-88M views each. The briefs ask for polished edits, not raw reels,
  and one asked for an old-school montage.

## Editing
- Hook in the first 1-2 s. For gaming on TikTok, the action hook (crosshair + enemy on screen, the play starts
  immediately) beats the text hook. If nothing happens in the first 2 s, start later.
- Text hooks: 3-5 words, large, one idea. Do not cover the action.
- Common effects, used on hits only: shake, zoom punch, flash, slow motion, radial blur, speed ramps. Restraint
  matters: use one clean transition, not many. A quick zoom resets attention when a shot runs long.
- Burned-in, animated captions are the norm in viral-tier clips (vendor dataset, ~80%).
- Reframing: follow crosshair and kill feed rather than a dumb centre crop. Our centre 3:4 crop keeps the crosshair.

## Music
- Gaming edits lean on slowed / Brazilian phonk with heavy bass: MONTAGEM-style tracks, Kordhell "Murder In My Mind",
  scraizis "RAGE", "CÓSMICO (Slowed)". TikTok's own trending gaming sounds list is the live source.
- Important: trending sounds must be added inside TikTok (licensed library), not burned into our file. Our renders
  carry the game audio plus a synthesized bed; the owner can lay a trending sound at low volume in the TikTok editor.
  Campaign rules still apply: original audio must stay clearly audible.

## Captions, hashtags, SEO
- TikTok search reads captions, on-screen text (claimed OCR) and spoken audio. Put the keyword ("MW4 multiplayer",
  "Modern Warfare 4") in the caption and the on-screen hook.
- Hashtags are context, not the main lever. Use 3-5 relevant ones: #mw4 #modernwarfare4 #callofduty #codclips
  #gaming (plus the campaign's required disclosure, #Sponsored, on its own line).
- Avoid generic #fyp spam; there is no evidence it helps.

## Growth
- New accounts sit at ~100-300 views until TikTok learns the audience. Single-topic accounts grow fastest.
- Post 1-3 times a day consistently (Later); gains plateau around 5+ posts a week in another dataset (Buffer).
- Engagement rate benchmark: ~3.85% by views (H1 2026); small accounts (1-5K followers) ~4.4%.
- Do not delete and repost; reply to comments in the first hour.

## Gaps vs our clips so far (apply to the next batch)
1. More beat-synced edits, cut to a phonk-style tempo (the user adds the licensed sound in TikTok if desired).
2. Open on the action within 1 s; drop text-only openings where the action is weak.
3. Keyword in the on-screen hook ("MW4 multiplayer ...") for TikTok search.
4. Add slow motion + shake on the single best hit of each clip (now supported).
5. Hashtags: #mw4 #modernwarfare4 #callofduty #codclips + #Sponsored line.
